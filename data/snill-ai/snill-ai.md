## Overview

Snill AI is an AI-powered no-code platform that generates complete business systems from plain-language descriptions, including relational databases, dashboards, workflows, REST APIs, and webhooks.

## Features

- Natural language to business system generation
- Relational database
- Dashboards
- Workflows
- REST API
- Webhooks
- Free plan: 2 apps, 1,000 records, 10 AI requests/day

## Pricing

Free plan for solo operators includes 2 apps, 1,000 records, and 10 AI requests per day. Paid plans for teams and higher usage.
