## Overview

Bonsai is a managed Elasticsearch service that provides search and analytics capabilities as a fully managed cloud database. Users do not need to operate their own Elasticsearch clusters.

## Features

- Managed Elasticsearch clusters
- 1 GB memory and 1 GB storage on the free tier
- Fully managed cloud deployment
- Search and analytics workloads

## Pricing

A free tier is available with 1 GB memory and 1 GB storage. Paid plans scale beyond the free tier limits.
