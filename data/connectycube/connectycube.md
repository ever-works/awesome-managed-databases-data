## Overview

ConnectyCube is a BaaS platform focused on real-time communication features including chat, voice and video calls, file attachments, and push notifications for mobile and web applications.

## Features

- Unlimited chat messages
- P2P voice & video calls
- File attachments
- Push notifications
- Free for apps up to 1000 users

## Pricing

Free tier available for apps with up to 1000 users. Paid plans for larger applications.
