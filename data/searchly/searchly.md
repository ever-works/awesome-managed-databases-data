## Overview

Searchly is a managed search service that provides indexed search capabilities as a hosted NoSQL database. It handles search index infrastructure so users do not need to manage their own search servers.

## Features

- Managed search indices
- 2 indices and 20 MB storage on the free plan
- Hosted search platform
- Search indexing and querying

## Pricing

A free plan is available with 2 indices and 20 MB storage. Paid plans scale beyond the free tier limits.
