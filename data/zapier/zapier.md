## Overview

Zapier is an automation platform that connects web applications and services to automate workflows without code.

## Features

- Connect thousands of apps
- Multi-step workflows (zaps)
- Webhooks
- Five zaps every 15 minutes (free)
- 100 tasks/month (free)
- Five active automations (free)

## Pricing

Free plan includes five zaps every 15 minutes, 100 tasks/month, and five active automations. Paid plans for higher task volumes and premium apps.
