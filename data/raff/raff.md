## Overview

Raff is a managed database service supporting PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Valkey.

## Features

- Managed PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Valkey
- 1 free database per account with no expiry
- 1 vCPU, 1 GB RAM, 2 GB storage
- Valkey: 512 MB memory
- 20 direct and 200 pooled connections on PostgreSQL
- TLS on every database
- Auto-pauses after 7 days without connections, resumes in about a minute
- No egress fees

## Pricing

1 free database per account with no expiry.
