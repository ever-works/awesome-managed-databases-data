## Overview

OneSignal is a customer engagement platform providing push notifications, email, SMS, and in-app messaging for mobile and web applications.

## Features

- Unlimited free push notifications
- 10,000 email sends per month
- Unlimited contacts
- Auto Warm Up for email deliverability
- In-app messaging
- SMS

## Pricing

Free tier includes unlimited push notifications and 10,000 email sends/month. Paid plans for higher volumes and advanced features.
