## Overview

IBM Db2 is a managed relational database service provided by IBM Cloud. It offers a free tier with 100MB of data storage as part of IBM Cloud's free tier offerings.

## Features

- Managed relational database service
- 100MB data storage on free tier
- Part of IBM Cloud

## Pricing

Freemium model. Free tier includes 100MB of data storage. Paid tiers available beyond free tier limits.

## Deployment

Cloud (IBM Cloud)
