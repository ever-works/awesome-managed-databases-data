## Overview

Deep Lake is an open-source database built for storing, querying, and managing complex AI data such as images, audio, video, and embeddings.

## Features

- Open-source database for complex AI data
- Store, query, and manage images, audio, and embeddings
- Tensor database for AI workloads
- Versioning and querying of unstructured data
- Integration with popular AI frameworks
