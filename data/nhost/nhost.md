## Overview

Nhost provides a free-tier PostgreSQL database service suitable for hobby projects, prototypes, and experiments.

## Core Specifications

| Specification | Value |
|---|---|
| Storage | 1 GB |
| CPU | 0.5 |
| RAM | 256 MiB |
| Backups | Not included |

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Network | 5 GB egress per month |
| Other Limits | 1 project, 1 member |
| Pause Policy | Paused after 1 week of inactivity |
