## Overview

TDengine is a high-performance time-series database and historian built for industrial data and IoT workloads.

## Features

- High-performance time-series database with industrial data management
- Built-in stream analytics
- AI-driven anomaly detection
- Purpose-built for operational environments
- High compression and efficient storage
